About This Project

This WebGIS project was developed as a spatial visualization platform for examining the distribution and temporal dynamics of marine ecosystems, particularly coral reefs and seagrass.

The platform combines GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, satellite imagery, and web-based visualization to make marine spatial information easier to explore and communicate.

Future development includes integration of ocean current information, interactive spatial layers, metadata management, and additional environmental datasets.
