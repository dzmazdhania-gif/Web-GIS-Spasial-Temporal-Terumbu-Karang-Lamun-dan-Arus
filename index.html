About This Project

This WebGIS project was developed as an interactive spatial visualization platform for exploring marine ecosystem distribution and temporal changes.

The platform integrates GIS, remote sensing, satellite basemaps, ecosystem mapping products, and model-based future projections into a single interactive spatial interface.

Observed/reference ecosystem information is distinguished from projected future scenarios to maintain a clear separation between mapped observations and model-based predictions.
